---
name: Sales strategy boundaries
description: Distinguish automatic sales guidance from voluntary commerce and preserve owner corrections.
---

Strategy action permissions constrain automatic commercial suggestions, not a visitor's voluntary use of the catalogue or factual order tracking.

**Why:** Blocking independent purchases or post-payment support would turn a consultative strategy into a restriction on essential existing functionality.

**How to apply:** Validate automatic CTAs against approved strategy and real capabilities; keep explicit product, quantity and payment confirmation in checkout. Never treat a generated reply as transaction authorization.

Owner corrections must not silently disappear when the visitor changes preferences.

**Why:** Corrections can represent verified context unavailable to the model, while later visitor statements may legitimately conflict.

**How to apply:** Preserve the correction and expose conflicting later statements for owner review, rather than treating either as an untraceable overwrite.

Transient chat UI such as product shelves and action buttons must represent the current visitor turn, not accumulated commercial memory.

**Why:** A remembered recommendation or strategy objective can otherwise make unrelated replies such as dates and times repeatedly show products or CTAs, distracting from the active conversation and making the assistant feel automated.

**How to apply:** Clear transient UI when a new turn starts, return empty/none explicitly, and only show products or actions when the current message expresses the matching intent. Keep voluntary catalogue access available elsewhere.

Contact consent is permission, not perpetual intent to leave the conversation.

**Why:** A persistent WhatsApp card competes with a later checkout, comparison or support response and recreates contact-first conversion even when the server policy is contextual. Offer it for the capture turn or a current WhatsApp action, not merely because a saved contact exists. Automatic sector templates are not an explicit owner's choice to prioritise contact.

Qualification and handoff should reflect the decision reached in this turn.

**Why:** Asking “faixa de investimento” after a declared budget is still repetition, even with different wording. A summary assembled before missing-data and action selection can send the owner an obsolete next step. Respect equivalent concepts and owner-authored facts, and finalize the factual handoff only after the server decision.

Measure observed requests separately from fulfilment or inferred intent.

**Why:** Asking for a visit does not confirm a reservation; silence is not evidence of abandonment. Keep request/continuation/contact events content-free, retain payment and WhatsApp-click authority in their existing flows, and do not label a suggested action as completed.

Resolve overlapping intent at clause level, and ground implicit commitments in the approved catalogue.

**Why:** “Não comprar, só comparar” rejects a purchase, not the conversation. “Fico com [oferta]” can be a purchase without the verb comprar, but generic “quero saber” cannot. A specifically requested, consented WhatsApp channel must outrank the broader human-handoff category; permission to use a channel and intent to use it are both required.

The assistant speaks as a member of the business and actively guides each qualified journey to one validated next step.

**Why:** Referring to “the responsible team” as a third party or repeatedly saying confirmation is pending makes the assistant passive and leaves the visitor without a concrete action.

**How to apply:** After answering, guide product intent to catalogue/checkout, a complete visit/appointment preference to structured contact consent when contact is absent, and an owner request to the business’s public WhatsApp when available. Respect explicit channel refusal and never call an unconfirmed visit confirmed.
