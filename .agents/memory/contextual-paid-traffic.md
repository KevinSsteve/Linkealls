---
name: Contextual paid traffic
description: Boundaries between simple acquisition links, legacy paid campaigns, lead attribution, and AI context.
---

Simple paid-traffic creatives are an acquisition-link feature, not a new state of the legacy media-buying campaign model. Keep their storage, lifecycle, and routes independent from payment, budget, provider publishing, and historical campaign obligations.

**Why:** The legacy campaign model carries financial and external-publishing state. Reusing it for a simple media-and-description link risks reactivating charging or provider side effects and makes old financial history harder to preserve.

**How to apply:** Public URLs may carry only an opaque creative slug plus allowlisted UTM fields. Resolve creative content server-side within the business scope, persist the trusted snapshot on the lead, and tell the AI that this context never overrides catalog, price, stock, checkout, authorization, or business-profile facts.
